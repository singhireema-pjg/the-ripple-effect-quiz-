⛏️ Beat the Clock: Twist Mission

Minecraft-inspired • 1 student • Decision Making • 10 minutes

🎯 Your Mission

Night is coming. You have 10 minutes to prepare your new Minecraft world.

You can complete only TWO tasks. But the world may surprise you.

Your job: make the best decision with the information you have.

10:00

ROUND 1 — Choose TWO

You have 30 seconds to plan.

🐺 TWIST 1 — Danger!

You hear a noise behind you.

A group of hostile mobs is moving toward your area.

7 minutes left

Do you keep your original plan or change it?

🌧️ TWIST 2 — Sudden Storm!

Dark clouds appear and a heavy storm begins.

Your shelter is still unfinished.

5 minutes left

What should be your priority now?

💎 TWIST 3 — Rare Opportunity!

You suddenly spot a small diamond vein nearby!

It could be very valuable.

3 minutes left

Do you take the opportunity or stick with your priority?

🏆 Mission Complete

🧠 What did you practise?

⏱️ Limited time → 🎯 Set priorities → 🤔 Make a choice → 🔄 Reassess when things change → ⚠️ Think about consequences

Teacher Reflection

  • What was your main goal?
  • Which twist changed your thinking the most?
  • Did you ever choose something valuable but decide it was not the priority?
  • What would make you change your decision?
